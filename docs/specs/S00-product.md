# S00 — Product specification
Status: PROPOSED | v0.1 | 2026-11-24

## North star
Maximize users' factual visibility into their productions, effects and evolution; expose blind spots and gaps against explicit objectives and direct effort toward demonstrably useful work at every scale. Preserve independent factual observation even when no objective is selected.

## Users and outcomes
An individual or authorized team can explore a chronological evidence graph, inspect the basis and limits of judgments, compare demonstrated capabilities and results against a dated target, and decide what evidence or improvement is needed next. Initial use cases: personal career evidence and CV; project/portfolio observability; cross-project learning and improvement. Longer-term: continuous feedback across an ecosystem and identity.

## Scope
GitHub/Git first, historical backfill plus continuous observations. Seven scales from artifact through identity (S01). Multiple independent dimensions: production, delivery, quality, architecture, security, reliability, collaboration, efficiency, utility and learning. Goals and reference frames are orthogonal to observation; external market, standards and user evidence may later be added. Useful work is not synonymous with activity, volume, tests passed or merge count. No forced scalar score, leaderboard or intrinsic rating of a person.

## Requirements
P1 Inspect factual observations, their provenance, coverage and unknowns.
P2 Navigate time and typed relationships across the seven scales without double counting.
P3 Distinguish activity, output, outcome, utility, costs and attribution.
P4 Compare observed state with explicit, versioned, dated reference frames and goals; retain conflicting evidence and missing data.
P5 Distinguish capability, evidence, observation and strategic gaps; do not classify missing proof as missing skill. Recommend candidate next steps with expected benefit, effort and evidence needed; user decides.
P6 Reconstruct projections from durable evidence; support duplicates, late arrival and correction.
P7 Keep private evidence private, including third-party contributions; publication requires explicit policy and approval.
P8 Independent consumers (CV, portfolio, orchestration/composition, improvement documents) do not own raw evidence or acquire execution permissions.
P9 Minimize time to first useful observation and cost of ongoing observation; measure before optimizing.

## Acceptance and priorities
UC-001 first: trace a single PR and its verification, with explicit unknown outcome. Then a goal-relative gap vertical; then live ingestion/reconciliation, historical coverage and multi-scale views. Backfill may proceed in parallel. Success requires factual precision, replay integrity and a decision-useful output, not number of features. Establish empirical baselines before numerical SLOs. No automatic hiring decisions, identity surveillance or autonomous publication.
